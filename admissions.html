General Admission Requirements

1. Undergraduate Bachelor's Degree Requirements

To be considered for undergraduate admission, an applicant must have:

  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE): At least two (2) principal passes obtained at the same sitting in relevant subjects.
  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE): At least five (5) credit passes, including English Language and Mathematics.
  • Diploma Holders: A recognized diploma in a related discipline with at least a Second Class / Credit grade from an accredited institution.
  • International Credentials: Cambridge A-Levels (minimum 2 passes), International Baccalaureate (minimum 24 points), or certified high school equivalence from UNEB.

2. Postgraduate & Master's Degree Requirements

Applicants for Postgraduate programmes (MBA, MPH, MSc) must present:

  • A Bachelor's degree with at least Lower Second Class Honours (or GPA 2.8+) from an accredited university.
  • Official undergraduate academic transcripts and degree certificate.
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV) and two professional/academic recommendation letters.
  • Minimum 2 years relevant work experience (for Executive MBA and MBA tracks).

3. Diploma & Certificate Programmes Requirements

Requirements for 2-year Diploma tracks:

  • UACE with at least one (1) principal pass and two (2) subsidiary passes.
  • UCE with at least three (3) credit passes.
