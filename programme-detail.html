Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Master algorithms, full-stack software architecture, cloud platforms, and AI engineering in a high-demand tech curriculum.

Programme Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at NileBridge University is calibrated to produce world-class software engineers, systems architects, and technology innovators.

Students develop deep competencies in algorithmic problem solving, distributed computing, database management, DevOps pipelines, and machine learning while working in state-of-the-art incubation labs.

Curriculum Structure

Semester 1

  • Introduction to Computer Science & Python Programming
  • Discrete Mathematics for Computing
  • Computer Architecture & Hardware Organization
  • Communication Skills & Academic Writing
  • Digital Logic Design

Semester 2

  • Object-Oriented Programming (Java & C++)
  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Linear Algebra & Calculus for Computing
  • Web Technologies & UI/UX Design
  • Database Management Systems (SQL & NoSQL)

Semester 1

  • Software Engineering Principles & Agile Methodologies
  • Operating Systems & Concurrency
  • Computer Networks & Protocols
  • Advanced Algorithms Analysis
  • Mobile Application Development (Flutter / React Native)

Semester 2

  • Cloud Computing & Microservices Architecture
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Fundamentals
  • Distributed Systems & Parallel Programming
  • Software Testing & Quality Assurance

Admission Requirements

Direct Entry (High School Leavers)

Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least two principal passes in Mathematics and Physics (or Economics/Chemistry/Biology), plus credit in English and Math at O-Level (UCE).

Diploma Entry (Mature & Fast-Track)

Recognized Diploma in Computer Science, IT, Electrical Engineering, or Telecommunications with at least a Credit class (admits directly to Year 2).

International Applicants

International Baccalaureate (IB), Cambridge A-Levels, or equivalent foreign secondary qualification evaluated by UNEB.
