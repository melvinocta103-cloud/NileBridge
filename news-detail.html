NileBridge Unveils $2.5M State-of-the-Art Artificial Intelligence & Robotics Hub

Office of Communications August 14, 2026 4 min read

JINJA TECH PARK — In a landmark advancement for science and technology education in East Africa, NileBridge University has officially commissioned its cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Laboratory.

Funded through an international technology consortium partnership, the $2.5 million hub features high-performance GPU clusters, humanoid robotics testbeds, autonomous systems simulation bays, and dedicated incubation spaces for student ventures.

"This laboratory is not just a building; it is a catalyst for African ingenuity. Our scholars will solve local challenges in agriculture, logistics, healthcare, and finance through world-class machine learning."
— Prof. Grace N. Tumusiime, Vice Chancellor

Undergraduate and postgraduate students in Computer Science, Software Engineering, and Civil Engineering will gain direct hands-on access to the facility starting this September 2026 semester.
