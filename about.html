About NileBridge University

Pioneering high-impact higher education, innovative research, and ethical leadership development since 1998.

Our Mission

To cultivate visionary thinkers, ethical professionals, and tech innovators who drive socio-economic transformation across Africa and the global landscape.

Our Vision

To be the premier digital-first, research-intensive university in Africa renowned for academic rigor, innovation ecosystems, and graduate employability.

Core Values

Excellence · Integrity · Innovation · Inclusivity · Global Impact. Anchoring every academic programme and student endeavor.

Our Heritage & Milestones

1998

Foundation of Nile Institute of Technology

Established with an initial cohort of 120 students focused on applied computing and commercial management sciences.

2008

Award of Full University Charter by NCHE

Granted full presidential charter and accreditation as NileBridge University, establishing the School of Law and Faculty of Health Sciences.

2018

Expansion & Jinja Innovation Park

Inauguration of the Jinja Tech Park, bringing robotics, software development, and renewable energy research facilities.

2026

Digital Admissions & Institutional Platform

Launch of the upgraded digital admissions and student portal, serving over 12,000 active scholars across 3 campuses.
